# What I did today
Still in the vein of purging my brain of the strict imperative worldview of most modern programming languages, I looked at 
another paradigm today, another method people get computers to solve problems: *Logic Programming*

In a logic programming language, your code consists of... sentences?

Logic programming has three components: 
- facts: fundamental truths,
- rules: relationships between facts, and
- queries: questions you ask the system.

So you declare facts and rules in your code and the computer uses logical inference and automated reasoning to answer queries.

I've gotten started with Prolog, the most popular logic programming language. I'm also making sure I solve at least one Haskell exercism exercise per day.
